- Full Johto story and Kanto postgame from HGSS, including the Kimono Girls and Eusine
- Following Pokémon
- Overworld background Pokémon allow you to easily see the notable encounters on each route
- Overworld background Pokémon in cities, towns, or areas with no encounters are just for the vibes
- Day/Night System with variable encounters 
- Dynamic overworld palettes
- HGSS Music
- Highly varied trainer teams and encounters, based on Crystal Legacy
- Pokémon that *can* learn an HM do not need to have learned it to use the HM Field Move
- Timekeeping does not track days of the week, everything is progression based
- 16 Gym leader rematches
- Two Safari Zones
- Unique surfing Pokémon sprites
- Customizable shiny rate
- Physical/special split is toggleable
- Fairy type is toggleable
- AutoRun and FastSurf are toggleable
- Quick run from wild battles using button combination
- Ball prompts for quick catching
- ... and much more!

# Customization Options

## Gamemode
- Choose between our "Recommended" preset or your "Custom" settings.
- **Pokemon Movepool**: Choose between Vanilla Gen 3 learnsets or Gen 7 learnsets with HnS-specific changes.
- **Physical/Special** Split: Choose between Gen 1-3 pre-split mechanics or Gen 4+ split mechanics.
- **Add Fairy Type**: For Pokémon *and Moves* (new in 2.0) that gained the Fairy type in Gen 6+, choose between their old or new typing.
- **Reusable TMs**: Choose between finite or infinite TMs. <!--This also impacts any one-time move tutors, if any.-->
- **Nature Mints**: Choose between being able to buy Mints in the Goldenrod Flower Shop either after Badge 3 or after completing the game.
- **Recharge Moves**: For recharge being skipped when fainting an opposing Pokemon, choose between Gen 1 and Gen 3 behavior.
- **Synchronize**: Choose between the Gen 3 50% chance or the Gen 8+ 100% chance to force a wild Pokemon's nature.
- **Sturdy**: Choose if this ability works like in Gen 3 or Gen 5+.
- **Sitrus Berry**: Choose if Sitrus Berries heal the Gen 3 30 HP or the Gen 4+ 25% HP. 
- **Legendary Abilities**: Choose between certain legendaries having "Pressure" like in Gen 3 or a better ability.
- **Survive Poison**: Choose between overworld poison tick damage causing your Pokémon to faint like in Gen 3 or to survive at 1hp.

## Features
- **Clock Type**: Choose between using your platform's Real Time Clock or using the ROM's fake RTC where 1 day passes ingame for every 1h of gameplay.
- **Shiny Chance**: Choose between 1/8192 (Emerald default), 1/4096 (Gen 6+), 1/2048, 1/1024, or 1/512.
- **Alt Shiny**: Choose between vanilla Shiny Palettes or custom Modern Emerald Shiny Palettes
- **Item Drops**: If enabled, defeated wild Pokémon's held items will be dropped and obtained by the player. No need to catch Pokemon or use Thief or Covet!
- **Frontier Bans**: Choose between banning or unbanning legendary and mythical Pokemon in the Battle Frontier.

## Randomizer
- Choose between Randomizer options being available or not
- **Starter Pokemon**: Choose if the three Starter options will be random or not.
- **Wild Pokemon**: Choose if wild encounter tables will be randomized.
- **Map Seeded**: Choose if randomized wild encounter tables are consistent per-map or everchanging.
- **Trainer**: Choose if trainer parties will be randomized.
- **Static Pokemon**: Choose if wild Pokemon encounters triggered by an OW interaction will be randomized.
- **Balancing**: Choose if Random Pokemon are limited to the same evolution stage as the base-game Pokemon they replace.
- **Legendaries**: Choose if Random legendary Pokemon can replace base-game Pokemon.
- **Type**: Choose if species typings will be randomized.
- **Moves**: Choose if moves (and therefore learnsets) will be randomized.
- **Abilities**: Choose if abilities will be randomized.
- **Evolutions**: Choose if evolution methods will be randomized.
- **Evo Lines**: Choose if the species each species evolves into will be randomized.
- **Effectiveness**: Choose if the type chart will be randomized.
- **Items**: Choose if obtainable items will be randomized. <!-- does this include wild mon held items? -->
- **Chaos Mode**: If enabled, your seed is rerolled every time a random value is generated. Outcomes are completely inconsistent and unpredictable.

## Nuzlocke
- **Nuzlocke**: Choose between Off, Easy (add permadeath), Normal (adds catch limits), or Hard (deletes save if you wipe).
- **Dupes Clause**: If enabled, species already caught do not count against the catch limits.
- **Shiny Clause**: If enabled, Shiny Pokemon can always be caught.
- **Nicknames**: If enabled, forces player to nickname every pokemon.
- **Fainting**: Choose if fainted Pokemon are trapped in the PC Cemetery or automatically released.
- **Infinite Rare Candy**: Only available when Nuzlocke enabled.

## Difficulty
- **Party Limit**: Choose any number between the minimum 1 Party Slot to the default 6 Party slots.
- **Level Cap**: Choose between Off, Easy (cap set to next Gym's highest level), or Hard (cap set to next Gym's lowest level).
- **Exp. Multiplier**: Choose between x1, x1.5, x2, or x0
- **Player Items**: If disabled, the player cannot use items during battle.
- **Trainer items**: If disabled, enemy trainers cannot use items during battle.
- **Player IVs**: Choose between Yes (normal behavior), No (all obtained Pokémon IVs set to 31), or "No (HP)" (all IVs set between 30 and 31 to allow for different Hidden Powers).
- **Trainer IVs**: Choose between Off (normal behavior), Scale (trainer Pokemon IVs universally increase as you obtain Gym Badges), and Hard (all trainer Pokemon have perfect IVs).
- **Player EVs**: If disabled, the player's Pokemon will not gain EVs by any means.
- **Trainer EVs**: Choose between Off (trainer Pokemon have no EVs), Scale (trainer Pokemon EVs universally increase as you obtain Gym Badges), and Hard (all trainer Pokemon have high EVs).
- **Less Escapes**: If enabled, the player is generally prevented from running from wild battles.
- **Escape Rope / Dig**: If disabled, the player cannot use Escape Ropes or Dig to exit dungeons.

## Challenges
- **Pokemon Center**: If disabled, normal healing locations cannot be utilized.
- **Ultra Expensive**: Choose between shop price multipliers of x1, x5, x10, or x50.
- **Evolution Limits**: Choose between Off (normal behavior), First (Pokemon can only evolve once after being obtained), or All (Pokemon cannot be evolved).
- **One Type Only**: Choose between Off, any available Type, or Random (which will choose a type for your run). Can only catch and use Pokemon of the selected Type.
- **BST Equalizer**: Choose between Off, 100, 255, or 500
- **Mirror Mode**: If enabled, the player get a copy of the enemy trainer's party for trainer battles.
- **Mirror Mode Thief**: If enabled, the player keeps the most recently battled trainer's party between battles.
